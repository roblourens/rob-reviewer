## Performance regression fixes detected (1)

### 1. repeated-work

**Fix location:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/snippets/browser/snippetCompletionProvider.ts:224` (`RIGHT`)  
**Blamed base line:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/snippets/browser/snippetCompletionProvider.ts:209`  
**Introducing path:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/snippets/browser/snippetCompletionProvider.ts`  
**Introducing commit:** `a5698e8857e8a7a934c5b285bcc138f95e83abf1`  
**Mechanism family:** `repeated-work`  
**Performance category:** `cpu`  
**Confidence:** 0.99

**Mechanism:** Fresh snippet-completion requests scan word starts and non-word columns from the beginning of the line to the cursor. Minified, punctuation-heavy lines produce O(cursor-column) repeated word-regex searches and anchor objects, followed by nested matching against available snippets, all synchronously on the renderer thread.

**Symptom:** Strong static evidence of typing/completion latency and renderer stalls that grow with cursor distance on long minified lines: the pre-fix hot path performs line-length-proportional repeated regex work before yielding. The added call-count test directly targets this mechanism; no timing measurements were performed in this review.

**Fixed behavior:** Retrieve the same snippets as before, find their maximum prefix length in one lightweight pass, and generate anchors only within that distance of the current word end. Word-regex lookup count and anchor allocation/matching multiplicity are bounded by snippet-prefix length rather than cursor distance along the line. No additional snippet-file reads are introduced.

**Evidence:** The deleted base line 209 starts the anchor loop at column 1. On punctuation-heavy lines it performs a word lookup and creates an anchor at a substantial fraction of columns before the current word, then provideCompletionItems tests these anchors inside its loop over snippets. TextModel.getWordAtPosition delegates to TokenizationTextModelPart.getWordAtPosition and getWordAtText, which repeatedly executes the word regex in a window capped at 1000 characters. SuggestModel's typing/trigger paths call provideSuggestionItems; that invokes this provider in the renderer and awaits its result before returning completion items. The added firstColumn at head line 222 and changed loop at 224 bound this repeated synchronous work by the maximum snippet prefix length. isPatternInWord at filters.ts:631-643 requires exhausting the anchor string, so longer anchors cannot match. The new 80,004-character-line test at snippetsService.test.ts:573-592 explicitly asserts fewer than 10 total word lookups across two requests. Blame of the old loop returns commit a5698e8857e8a7a934c5b285bcc138f95e83abf1 and the same origin path.

Previously punctuation-heavy text generated O(C) word lookups/anchors and nested matching across S snippets. The new scan examines at most a P-sized column window, plus terminal anchors, and adds one O(S) prefix-length pass. Whole-line lowercasing remains O(L), unchanged. With S=0 the new anchor loop does no word lookups.
- Effective concurrency: Quick suggestions are delayed/coalesced by the existing scheduler; retriggers cancel the previous request token. The snippet provider still does not observe cancellation internally. Each fresh request runs its bounded CPU scan serially on the renderer, parallel in scheduling with other completion providers; no additional per-file boundary calls are created by overlapping requests.
