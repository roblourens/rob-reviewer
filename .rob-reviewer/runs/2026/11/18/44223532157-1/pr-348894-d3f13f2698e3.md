### Scenario 1: Cold and warm Copilot runtime acquisition, including scoped virtual/credential clients

- Critical path: CopilotAgent.\_ensureClient acquires the runtime before session launch. Certificate loading and startup asset resolution precede client.start; independent runtime, ripgrep, telemetry, and optional plugin lookups overlap.
- Scaling input: Number of distinct scoped chats requiring clients, versus many ordinary chats sharing one client; bounded platform candidates.
- Effective concurrency: Normal concurrent callers share one startup/retry sequence. Different scoped clients can start independently, as before. The five asset checks within each candidate run concurrently.
- Cache behavior: Existing \_client, \_clientStarting, and \_ensureClientHealing retain/coalesce normal acquisition. ScopedCopilotClient.starting is shared per chat. Failed starts clear their handles; config-change retries remain bounded.
- Mechanism confidence: High confidence in unchanged host process cardinality and startup ordering from the complete client-options diff and acquisition callers. SDK internal initialization is outside the available implementation.
- Magnitude uncertainty: No timings or inspection of the newly published SDK/native runtime implementation; startup changes internal to that dependency cannot be established from its version number.
- Expensive boundaries:
  - filesystem at <code>src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/copilot/copilotRuntimePaths.ts:resolveCopilotRuntimePaths</code>: Resolve the SDK symlink and check runtime/native/extension assets.; cardinality: One realpath followed by five concurrent existence checks per candidate; at most two scope directories and two Linux platform candidates, with early success.
    - Introduced by diff: false; previous behavior: Identical candidate enumeration and filesystem operations under the &#64;github scope; the diff substitutes &#64;microsoft.; critical-path effect: Asset resolution must finish before the client starts.
  - subprocess at <code>src/vs/platform/agentHost/node/copilot/copilotAgent.ts:\_ensureClientOnce</code>: Start the SDK-owned runtime over spawned stdio.; cardinality: One process per acquired client, not per caller or tool. Normal sessions share a client; scoped clients are retained by chat key.

- Verdict: No reportable host-side regression: configuration is regrouped while acquisition, reuse, overlap, and cleanup ownership remain unchanged.
