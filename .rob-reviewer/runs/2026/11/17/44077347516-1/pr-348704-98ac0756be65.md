# Performance review: sessions: polish scheduled-message previews and inline editing

[PR #348704](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/348704) at `98ac0756be65c87b2bd0b144221dab2ae8f59dd4`

## Findings (1)

### 1. [medium] Release cached preview resources after the hover closes

**Finding ID:** `PERF-58D277482430`  
**Location:** <code>src/vs/workbench/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsList.ts:1965</code> (<code>RIGHT</code>)  
**Performance category:** <code>memory</code>  
**Mechanism family:** <code>cache-lifecycle</code>  
**Resource:** Detached preview DOM, ActionBars, per-message timers/listeners, and original automation/request snapshots including embedded attachment strings.  
**Scaling:** One stale card per previously hovered row template, bounded by the virtual list's peak template pool, not by all sessions ever visited. Each card retains all of that session's previewed requests and their attachment bytes; several image-bearing cards can therefore retain substantial memory.  
**Outcome:** Unnecessary long-lived renderer memory and continuing timer work after preview content is no longer needed.  
**PR causality:** <code>introduced</code>  
**Previous behavior:** The clock used a static text hover and retained no scheduled-request card, ActionBars, request snapshots, or per-message update timers.  
**Changed behavior:** Preview stores are now owned by the reusable row template, rather than the displayed hover. Closing the preview, cancelling its last request, and recycling the row do not release the cached card; only constructing a different card in that template or disposing the entire template does.  
**Confidence:** 0.95 — The cache's only disposal/replacement sites, row-template pooling, interval ownership, and request-to-automation closure chain are directly traceable. Retention does not depend on timing or a failed operation. Exact retained bytes depend on attachments and how many row templates were hovered.

**Causal diff evidence:** The added line registers card-store disposal only with template.disposables, and lines 1968-1970 cache the rendered card. The new empty-request branch at 1956-1957 returns without clearing that cache. Existing disposeElement only clears elementDisposables, while RowCache retains released templates until list disposal.

Hovering scheduled messages with screenshots or embedded selections and then cancelling them can leave their original attachment snapshots retained for the lifetime of the sessions list, even after their schedules have been deleted. Repeating this across row templates retains additional large snapshots and leaves hidden preview timers running.

**Evidence:** renderScheduledRequestsCard installs one minute interval and document visibility listener per timed message through renderScheduledRequestTime. Its action wrappers at sessionScheduledRequestsCard.ts:67 capture request and action; ScheduledChatsService.items creates those actions with closures over the original automation. Those descriptors can contain base64 image/file contents (scheduledChatRequest.ts:45-69). Consequently a cancelled/deleted request's original attachment data, ActionBars, detached DOM and timers remain reachable from the cached card after the hover is hidden.

**Suggested direction:** Keep a card alive across refreshes while its hover is open, but dispose its store and remove the cached entry when the preview is closed and no longer needed. Also invalidate stale entries on row-resource replacement or request removal; avoid retaining full request/action objects when only their IDs are needed.
