**Mechanism:** Dropping live probe replies during a pending relay handshake made the client watchdog dispose a reachable connection at 25s, aborting 25-30s handshakes and repeating serialized credential preparation, WebSocket connection/join setup and protocol recovery, while session requests remained gated.

**Symptom:** A reachable host whose reconnect response arrives at 27s could not complete that attempt despite replying to pings, delaying session recovery by another retry/backoff and network setup, or exhausting the retry policy.

**Fixed behavior:** Changed lines 487-492 admit a response only to an outstanding ping tagged with the current handshake ID on a joined to-client lane, excluding closed generations. This reaches the existing inbound-liveness reset without completing the handshake or rescheduling its 30s deadline, allowing a responsive slow reconnect to finish instead of triggering redundant recovery.

**Evidence:** The deleted base line 478 unconditionally discarded every non-handshake response while \_handshakeId was set. AgentHostProtocolClient.\_installTransport (756) routes onMessage to \_handleMessage, whose line 3296 resets inbound liveness; its \_onPingTimer (4108) already sends probes during reconnect, and ProtocolLivenessMonitor arms the close timer for 5s + 20s. Thus answered probes were discarded and a reconnect reply taking 27s was preempted at 25s. \_onLivenessTimeout (4122-4127) disposes the transport and \_attemptReconnect/\_scheduleReconnect (1196-1330) retries serially, repeating connection preparation, WebSocket joins, capability advertisement and reconnect RPC. Added integration test lines 996-1007 models a 27s reconnect response with immediate ping replies and asserts successful recovery on the first replacement socket; this is static and test-source evidence, not a benchmark run. blame\_base\_line identifies the unconditional discard as introduced by 431af7bbe286c90b2df828d495cf31e3e33f0fd7.
