- Cache behavior: Uses the already loaded account-wide catalog. No cache lookup, invalidation, or network retry is triggered by checkbox changes.

- Expensive boundaries: none found
- Verdict: No reportable regression: the added synchronous work is bounded by the visible catalog, user-paced, and lacks evidence of meaningful latency or jank at realistic cardinality.
