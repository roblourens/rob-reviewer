The changed await is on the test's critical path but replaces timer-driven macrotasks with one promise continuation.
- Scaling input: A single navigation request in this test. Before the diff, timer turns scaled with scheduler delay until the request arrived; after the diff, one promise wake-up serves the request.
- Effective concurrency: One input resolution and one fake navigation request are active. The cancellation race and fake reply promise overlap exactly as before; there is no queue, limiter, or aggregate concurrency.
- Cache behavior: No cache participates. Both cold and repeated test runs construct fresh fixtures and DeferredPromises; there are no keys, misses, negative states, or coalescing concerns.
- Mechanism confidence: High: the deleted loop scheduled timeout(0) repeatedly until the fake call assigned a callback, while the added DeferredPromise is completed by that same fake call and awaited once. The proxy and services are test doubles, so no real IPC, filesystem, database, network, or subprocess boundary is reached.
- Magnitude uncertainty: Exact CI savings vary with timer clamping and host load, but the change removes rather than adds repeated scheduling. Two DeferredPromise objects add only bounded per-test allocation.
- Expensive boundaries: none found
- Verdict: No performance regression. The diff removes unbounded repeated timer scheduling and preserves bounded cleanup: disposal cancels the race, then the test completes the underlying fake reply.
