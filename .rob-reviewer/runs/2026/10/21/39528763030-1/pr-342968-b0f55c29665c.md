### Scenario 3: A notarization returns MacSignFailed

- Critical path: The failed artifact waits three minutes and then must complete one retry before its task can succeed or fail. Other artifact chains continue independently, but top-level Promise.all still waits for all successful work or rejects on a terminal failure.
- Scaling input: Effective cardinality is the number of the 2 or 4 artifact groups that independently return the exact MacSignFailed code. Each such group receives at most one retry.
- Effective concurrency: One process per artifact chain remains the limit. A failed initial process exits before its timer and retry, so aggregate subprocess concurrency cannot exceed the existing two/four task count; there is no retry burst above the initial cap.
- Cache behavior: Failures are not cached or coalesced across artifact groups. The retry is intentionally fresh after a fixed delay; the two-attempt constant bounds repeated work.
- Mechanism confidence: High: the exact-code guard, constant attempt limit, timer, subprocess call, and top-level task cardinality directly establish the mechanism.
- Magnitude uncertainty: It is uncertain how often multiple artifact groups fail together and how long a retry takes, but the maximum repeat count and concurrency are explicit and small.
- Expensive boundaries:
  - subprocess at <code>build/azure-pipelines/darwin/codesign.ts:22-39</code>: Launch one additional Node sign.ts/dotnet ESRP notarization attempt for each artifact group with the targeted failure.; cardinality: At most one added subprocess chain per failing artifact: up to four on standard builds or two on universal builds, but normally only the artifact that failed.
    - Introduced by diff: true; previous behavior: The first failed notarization terminated the build; recovery required an external job rerun, which repeated broader signing/notarization work.; critical-path effect: Adds a fixed three-minute wait plus the retry duration to recovery or terminal-failure latency for that artifact.
  - network at <code>build/azure-pipelines/darwin/codesign.ts:22-39 via build/azure-pipelines/common/sign.ts:110-117,164-200</code>: Resubmit the already signed artifact group through ESRP/Apple notarization.; cardinality: One additional grouped remote submission per MacSignFailed artifact, bounded by NOTARIZE\_ATTEMPTS = 2.
    - Introduced by diff: true; previous behavior: No in-process resubmission occurred; the job failed and any manual job retry repeated all relevant phases.; critical-path effect: The retry is on the recovery critical path after the three-minute delay.
- Verdict: No reportable performance regression. The added boundary is narrowly triggered, limited to one retry per failed artifact, does not increase peak concurrency, and is the intentional recovery work replacing immediate failure/manual reruns. Persistent failures pay bounded extra diagnosis latency, not an unbounded or scaling regression.

**Summary:** Reviewed the complete one-file diff and traced standard, universal, successful, transient-failure, and persistent-failure paths through child-process and ESRP/Apple boundaries. The change adds only a fixed one-retry path for the targeted failure and does not increase successful-path work or peak concurrency; no high-confidence actionable performance issue was found.
