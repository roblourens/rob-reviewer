# Performance review: Engineering - enable automatic retries for failed Electron unit test files

[PR #343798](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/pull/343798) at `cbd070aad7550dc92590b6256557726c6b0a6880`

## Findings (1)

### 1. [medium] Preserve the 15-minute watchdog for hung attempts

**Finding ID:** `PERF-43FB77DBC5DF`  
**Location:** <code>.github/workflows/pr-linux-test.yml:311</code> (<code>RIGHT</code>)  
**Performance category:** <code>subprocess</code>  
**Mechanism family:** <code>cleanup-lifecycle</code>  
**Resource:** hosted CI runner time and live Electron subprocesses  
**Scaling:** The cost is a fixed additional 10 minutes for each hung platform invocation; with the same timeout increase on Linux, macOS, and Windows, aggregate waste can reach 30 runner-minutes per affected PR run.  
**Outcome:** increased failure-path CI latency and retained runner capacity  
**PR causality:** <code>introduced</code>  
**Previous behavior:** The Electron step was forcibly canceled after 15 minutes, bounding a no-progress test process and allowing the workflow to release its runner sooner.  
**Changed behavior:** A non-terminating Electron test invocation now retains the hosted runner and Electron process for up to 25 minutes before GitHub Actions cancels it, instead of 15 minutes.  
**Confidence:** 0.96 — The workflow timeout change is explicit, \`scripts/test.sh\` runs one Electron process synchronously, and \`index.js\` cannot enter its retry loop until the renderer emits \`all done\`. Thus a hung attempt receives no retry benefit from the extra ten minutes. The only uncertainty is how often the suite hangs, which affects severity rather than the mechanism.

**Causal diff evidence:** The added \`timeout-minutes: 25\` on the Electron test step replaces the previous 15-minute cap; the same increase is made in the Darwin and Windows PR workflows.

When an Electron test deadlocks or otherwise stops making progress, PR CI and all later steps in that platform job are delayed by an additional 10 minutes while the hosted runner and Electron processes remain occupied. The same change on all three OS workflows can consume up to 30 extra runner-minutes for a cross-platform hang.

**Evidence:** \`.github/workflows/pr-linux-test.yml:311\` raises the outer watchdog from 15 to 25 minutes. \`scripts/test.sh:47\` synchronously launches Electron, while \`test/unit/electron/index.js:628\` awaits the attempt's \`done\` promise and the renderer only sends \`all done\` after Mocha completes (\`test/unit/electron/renderer.js:487\`). Therefore a deadlocked renderer/test never reaches the new retry logic and simply occupies the runner until the enlarged workflow timeout expires.

**Suggested direction:** Keep the 25-minute total budget for completed retries, but add a per-attempt no-progress/watchdog timeout (at most the prior 15-minute bound) in the Electron runner, or otherwise separate retry headroom from the hang timeout so a stuck first attempt is terminated promptly.

### Scenario 3: Electron test attempt hangs or stops making progress

- Critical path: The shell waits synchronously for Electron; \`index.js\` awaits the renderer's \`all done\` event. A deadlock or no-progress renderer blocks all later job steps until the workflow timeout.
- Scaling input: Number of platform workflows experiencing a no-progress Electron invocation; the fixed increase is ten runner-minutes per platform.
- Effective concurrency: One stuck Electron process per affected platform workflow; Linux, macOS, and Windows workflows can retain runners independently. No retry executes because attempts are sequential and completion-gated.
- Cache behavior: A hung attempt never completes, so no retry selection, cache behavior, or coalescing is reached. The single renderer remains live until the outer workflow watchdog cancels it.
- Mechanism confidence: Very high: the outer timeout changed from 15 to 25 minutes, while retries are reached only after \`done\` resolves.
- Magnitude uncertainty: The frequency of full renderer/test deadlocks is unknown, but workflow timeouts exist for this realistic failure mode; the additional latency and runner occupancy are exactly ten minutes whenever it occurs.
- Expensive boundaries:
  - subprocess at <code>.github/workflows/pr-linux-test.yml:311</code>: Wait for the Electron test process under the enlarged workflow timeout.; cardinality: One non-terminating Electron invocation per affected platform, retained for up to 25 rather than 15 minutes.
    - Introduced by diff: true; previous behavior: GitHub Actions canceled and cleaned up the same no-progress invocation after 15 minutes.; critical-path effect: It blocks the remainder of that platform job and occupies its hosted runner for ten additional minutes.
  - network at <code>test/unit/electron/index.js:287</code>: Keep the local remote-resource server alive while awaiting test completion.; cardinality: One localhost test server and its associated Electron resources remain live with the stuck invocation.
    - Introduced by diff: false; previous behavior: The resources were retained only until the prior 15-minute cancellation bound.; critical-path effect: These resources remain retained until workflow cancellation; they do not enable progress.
- Verdict: Reportable medium-severity cleanup/latency regression: retry headroom was added by extending the only hang watchdog, although a hung attempt cannot use retries.
