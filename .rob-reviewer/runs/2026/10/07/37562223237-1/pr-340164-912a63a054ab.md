- Critical path: The main process constructs service wrappers without loading the native module. - Scaling input: Policy-definition count and renderer-window count. One native policy watcher is created for the product policy set; the main-process managed-settings service coalesces all window initialization requests onto one initialization promise and one watcher.
- Effective concurrency: NativePolicyService serializes watcher replacement through a Throttler. NativeManagedSettingsService coalesces non-forced initialization and serializes creation/replacement through a Throttler; separate renderer windows can issue IPC concurrently but do not create separate OS watchers.
- Cache behavior: Within a process, the managed-settings initialization promise is the warm-cache/coalescing state; repeated initialization reuses it. Equal managed-setting definition sets return the existing initialization. A new process starts cold and creates its own bounded watcher set. Failures clear the managed-settings initialization promise for an explicit retry.
- Mechanism confidence: High confidence in the unchanged repository-level load placement, watcher cardinality, coalescing, and critical paths; insufficient evidence of any regressive mechanism inside the upgraded artifact.
