## Performance regression fixes detected (1)

### 1. repeated-work

**Fix location:** `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsList.ts:4277` (`RIGHT`)  
**Blamed base line:** `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsList.ts:4275`  
**Introducing path:** `src/vs/sessions/contrib/sessions/browser/views/sessionsList.ts`  
**Introducing commit:** `e63efae792a941b3491de0e06afff672f7960859`  
**Mechanism family:** `repeated-work`  
**Performance category:** `rendering`  
**Confidence:** 0.98

**Mechanism:** Each provider/backend catalogue notification synchronously rebuilt the entire sessions tree, and one registration also fans out into related management and host-filter notifications. Startup catalogue hydration therefore repeated O(session-count) filtering/grouping/allocation and DOM-tree reconciliation for a provider-count-sized burst.

**Symptom:** Populated cached profiles incurred repeated full-list rendering during catalogue hydration, increasing startup settling latency and main-thread CPU/GC pressure.

**Fixed behavior:** The changed provider listener calls scheduleListUpdate(), which keeps one animation-frame disposable and merges related provider, host-filter, and empty catalogue invalidations; update() clears the queued work so an authoritative immediate render also cancels a redundant pending render.

**Evidence:** In the base revision, the provider-catalogue listener at line 4275 synchronously called SessionsList.update() for every onDidChangeProviders event. SessionsProvidersService fires that event once per provider/backend registration, AgentHostFilterService synchronously recomputes and may emit a related host-filter invalidation for the same registration, and SessionsManagementService emits a related session-catalogue event. SessionsList.update() performs collection-wide filtering/grouping/sorting, constructs the full tree input, and calls tree.setChildren. The added test drives 125 interleaved provider/catalogue/host invalidations and proves the changed path performs one session read and one update after a frame rather than one render per notification.
