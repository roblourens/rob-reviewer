**Mechanism:** Closing or unregistering an auxiliary window bypassed the sheet's sole close path, leaving the sheet DisposableStore, rendered DOM/body state, caller closures, and unresolved promise reachable for the remaining workbench lifetime.

**Symptom:** Auxiliary-window sheet teardown retained DOM nodes, listeners, body resources, and pending async continuations after the owning window closed.

**Fixed behavior:** The added filtered one-shot window lifecycle listener invokes onDismiss for the sheet's owning window, and close now removes the overlay synchronously while that window is closing, disposing listeners/body resources and resolving the pending sheet interaction instead of retaining them.

**Evidence:** Before this PR, buildMobileSheetShell registered all sheet DOM/window/viewport listeners in a DisposableStore but had no auxiliary-window teardown subscription; the only cleanup path was shell.close(), and callers such as showMobileContentSheet awaited a promise resolved only from close's animation callback. The added regression tests exercise unregister and beforeunload and require the auxiliary dialog/listeners to be released and the pending promise to resolve. blame attributes the base shell lifecycle to a51c7371fac13bbf9413c94ef7a2c6d6711de695.
