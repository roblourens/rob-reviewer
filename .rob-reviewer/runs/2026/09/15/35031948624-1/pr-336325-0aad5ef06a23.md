- Verdict: No reportable regression. The diff replaces an HTML fixture and native ResizeObserver with string content and one manual layout, reducing nondeterministic browser work without changing production paths.

### Scenario 3: Hover re-entry after a layout-suppressed mouseleave, followed by a second mouseleave
