### Scenario 3: Queued, hidden, cancelled, failed, navigated, or completed-without-progress submissions

- Critical path: The interaction terminates synchronously when the relevant lifecycle signal arrives, emits one termination event, and disposes response, widget, document, and animation-frame listeners. The underlying request behavior is intentionally unchanged.
- Scaling input: Cost is constant per user submission and does not follow deferred queue lifetime because queued measurements are immediately detached.
- Effective concurrency: Queued submissions end observation immediately without awaiting the deferred request. Cancellation/error/hide/disposal paths are idempotent through the active flag, preventing duplicate telemetry or cleanup.
- Cache behavior: No cache participation. Re-showing a hidden widget does not recreate or resume an interaction, so there is no repeated cold work.
- Mechanism confidence: High confidence from the active guard, disposal ownership, animation-frame cancellation, and focused lifecycle tests.
- Magnitude uncertainty: Telemetry backend batching cost is outside the inspected diff, but each payload is fixed-size and emitted once.
- Expensive boundaries:
  - serialization at <code>src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/chatUserInteractionTelemetry.ts:ChatUserInteraction.\_finish</code>: Emit a fixed-size termination telemetry event.; cardinality: Exactly one event per attempted foreground interaction regardless of repeated terminal signals.
    - Introduced by diff: true; previous behavior: These termination reasons were not emitted by this telemetry feature.; critical-path effect: Constant local bookkeeping at termination; no provider cancellation or extra remote call is introduced by the measurement.
- Verdict: No cleanup-lifecycle or retention finding: every terminal path releases observers and pending frames, and queued work is not retained.
