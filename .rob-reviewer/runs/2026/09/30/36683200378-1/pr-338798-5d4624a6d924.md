- Mechanism confidence: High: the fixture definitions, two-chat mocked render path, increased expected counts, larger viewport, and single local navigation are explicit in the changed code.
- Magnitude uncertainty: CI time and memory increase are not quantified, and generic fixture tooling may add incidental asset requests, but there is no product path and no evidence of an unbounded or severe test-throughput regression.
