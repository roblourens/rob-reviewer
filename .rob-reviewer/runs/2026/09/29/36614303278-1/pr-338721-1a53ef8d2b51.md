## Performance regression fixes detected (1)

### 1. repeated-work

**Fix location:** `src/vs/platform/agentHost/browser/agentHostProtocolClient.ts:1975` (`RIGHT`)  
**Blamed base line:** `src/vs/platform/agentHost/browser/agentHostProtocolClient.ts:1972`  
**Introducing path:** `src/vs/platform/agentHost/browser/remoteAgentHostProtocolClient.ts`  
**Introducing commit:** `f91a396d242c451b0e316ad5ccbc3dc19fbf96d0`  
**Mechanism family:** `repeated-work`  
**Performance category:** `network`  
**Confidence:** 0.96

**Mechanism:** The watchdog observed only complete protocol messages, so a live connection carrying a slowly arriving large response appeared silent for 25 seconds and was force-closed, causing reconnect and potentially repeating the transfer.

**Symptom:** Slow large agent-host responses could be interrupted by a false liveness timeout, adding reconnect latency and retransmission instead of completing.

**Fixed behavior:** The change propagates partial inbound-data activity through Web PubSub, SSH, dev-tunnel, relay, and reconnecting transports into `_recordInboundTraffic`; SSH/tunnel relay reports are throttled to once per second. Live slow downloads now refresh the existing watchdog without waiting for full message reassembly, avoiding false close/reconnect and repeated transfer.

**Evidence:** The base client updated `_lastReadTime` and reset both liveness timers only in `_handleMessage`, after a complete ProtocolMessage reached the renderer. Web PubSub reassembles up to 32 MiB from ~900 KiB segments, while SSH and dev-tunnel WebSockets can receive arbitrary stream chunks before emitting one complete message. During a response taking over the 5 s ping plus 20 s close budget, the watchdog therefore force-closed a live transport, discarded the in-progress response, and entered reconnect. Added tests exercise a one-minute response with bytes arriving every second and show that the old complete-message-only signal would cross the close budget.
