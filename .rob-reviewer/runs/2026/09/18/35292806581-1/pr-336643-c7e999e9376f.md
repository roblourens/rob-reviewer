## Performance regression fixes detected (1)

### 1. unbounded-retention

**Fix location:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/speechToText/chatSpeechToTextService.ts:1076` (`RIGHT`)  
**Blamed base line:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/speechToText/chatSpeechToTextService.ts:1070`  
**Introducing path:** `src/vs/workbench/contrib/chat/browser/speechToText/chatSpeechToTextService.ts`  
**Introducing commit:** `85264502e4c6c4db48604183dc78bee88807282d`  
**Mechanism family:** `unbounded-retention`  
**Performance category:** `memory`  
**Confidence:** 0.97

**Mechanism:** An initial on-device model error was ignored, allowing a recording to enter capture with no transcription session; every audio callback then crossed IPC and was retained in the utility process's `_pendingChunks` until manual stop or the 20-minute limit.

**Symptom:** Sustained dictation after an early model-load/download failure causes steadily increasing utility-process memory and needless PCM encoding/IPC, potentially retaining about 38 MB of audio over the maximum session before object overhead.

**Fixed behavior:** The added Error branch routes the initial snapshot through `_handleModelStatus`, which calls `_failModelSession`/`_failSession`; the changed generation invalidates startup, cancellation resets the utility-process pending chunk buffer, and capture is torn down before continuous PCM streaming can begin.

**Evidence:** In the base revision, `_startLocalSession` explicitly excluded an initial `Error` snapshot from `_trackModelPreparation`, so no status listener handled that terminal state and startup continued into `_startCapture`/Recording. The utility-process `_openSession` leaves `_session` unset after the model failure, while `pushAudio` copies every PCM16 chunk and appends it to `_pendingChunks` whenever `_sessionActive` is still true. At 16 kHz mono PCM16 this retains roughly 32 KB/s (about 38 MB over the 20-minute dictation limit, plus per-chunk overhead) and also performs continuous audio IPC despite transcription being impossible.
