## Findings (1)

### 1. [low] Avoid serializing the 10-second producer with window reload

**Finding ID:** `PERF-0D948084D8B4`  
**Location:** <code>test/smoke/src/areas/terminal/terminal-persistence.test.ts:50</code> (<code>RIGHT</code>)  
**Performance category:** <code>latency</code>  
**Mechanism family:** <code>eager-work</code>  
**Resource:** Subprocess timer time plus the Electron window reload/workbench restoration boundary.  
**Scaling:** The added serialized delay grows as \`count \* interval\` (100 \* 100 ms here), capped in incremental impact by the roughly nine seconds that previously overlapped reload; retries multiply the added wall time.  
**Outcome:** Increased smoke-test wall-clock latency and reduced CI throughput from lost overlap between terminal production and window reload.  
**PR causality:** <code>introduced</code>  
**Previous behavior:** The test waited only until at least 10 lines had arrived (about one second), initiated reload while the producer emitted the remaining 90 lines, and then waited for line 99, allowing reload and producer time to overlap.  
**Changed behavior:** The test now waits for all 100 rate-limited lines before invoking \`reloadWindow\`, serializing the producer's approximately 10-second run with the full window reload/workbench restore boundary.  
**Confidence:** 0.98 — The timer period and count make the pre-reconnect gate's minimum duration deterministic, and \`reloadWindow\` explicitly awaits a new window plus restored workbench. The diff clearly moves that boundary from after the old &gt;=10-lines gate (about 1 second) to after line 99 (about 10 seconds). Exact reload duration varies by CI machine, affecting magnitude but not the lost overlap.

**Causal diff evidence:** RIGHT line 50 adds \`await terminal.waitForTerminalText(...count - 1)\` before the reconnect branch. With 100 values emitted by the changed/retained \`setInterval(..., 100)\`, this gate cannot pass until roughly 10 seconds after launch; line 53's awaited \`reloadWindow\` can only begin afterward.

Every non-Linux smoke run of the window-reload case takes up to roughly nine seconds longer; failures can repeat this because the enclosing Terminal suite allows three retries, increasing CI wall time and reducing smoke-test throughput.

**Evidence:** \`runCommandInTerminal\` only writes the command and presses Enter, so the child Node process continues asynchronously. \`waitForTerminalText\` polls the terminal buffer until its predicate passes. \`Code.reloadWindow\` then awaits \`waitForWindowReload\` and \`whenWorkbenchRestored\`. Before the diff, reload started after about 10 emissions (~1 second) while the remaining ~9 seconds of output continued in the persistent terminal; final sequence waiting overlapped those operations. Now the pre-reconnect wait consumes the full ~10 seconds and only then starts reload, extending the smoke test by \`min(reload duration, ~9 seconds)\`.

**Suggested direction:** Preserve completed-output-before-reconnect semantics without the ten-second gate by emitting the initial 100-line sequence immediately (or with a much shorter interval), then entering raw-mode keepalive before awaiting completion and reloading.
